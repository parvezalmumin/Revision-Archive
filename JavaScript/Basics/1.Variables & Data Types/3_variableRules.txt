Variable Rules :-

-) Variable names are case sensitive; "a" and "A" is different
-) Only letters, digits, underscore(_) and $ sign is allowed. (Not even space)
-) Only a letter, underscore(_) or $ should be 1st character.
-) Rserved words cannot be variables names.
