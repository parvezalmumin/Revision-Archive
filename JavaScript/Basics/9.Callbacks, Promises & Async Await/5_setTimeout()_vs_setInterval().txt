                               setTimeout() vs setInterval()
______________________________________________________________________________________
| setTimeout()                              |          setInterval()                  |
|___________________________________________|_________________________________________|
|1| Executes once after a delay             | Repeats at intervals                    |
|_|_________________________________________|_________________________________________|
|2| Example: Show a message after 3 seconds | Example: Show a message every 3 seconds |
|_|_________________________________________|_________________________________________|
